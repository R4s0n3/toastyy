## How to play

Turn the knob to select difficulty and pull the lever. Wait until the orange
button changes from WAIT to STOP, then tap the screen or STOP before the toast
burns. Stops count only in the final 720 milliseconds. Early taps keep toasting
and earn nothing. A successful stop earns XP; a burnt slice earns 5 XP. DAYDREAM unlocks at 1,000,000 total XP.

## Sound and haptics

Both can be changed independently in Settings. Sound follows the iPhone's silent
mode. Haptics require a compatible physical device.

## Restore Remove Ads

Open Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought Remove Ads.
The purchase is permanent and is not a subscription. For billing or refunds,
visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com/.

## Privacy and advertising

Privacy policy: https://miomideal.com/privacy-policy
Where required, Advertising Privacy Choices is available in Settings.
To report an inappropriate advertisement, use its in-ad reporting controls or
contact support with a screenshot, advertiser name, and approximate time.

Game progress and records are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove
your local progress; restoring an App Store purchase does not restore game XP.
