Toastyy 1.0.0 (6)

New in build 6: live Toastyy privacy and support links in Settings.

What to test
• Larger toaster, readable XP and score, background-only paper grain.
• Camera smoothly centers the toaster when the lever goes down.
• WAIT becomes STOP only in the final 720 ms. Early taps must not stop or score.
• Miss the window and the toast burns.
• Try every difficulty, sound/haptics, app switching and relaunching.
• Verify XP and records remain after updating from an earlier build.
• Test Remove Ads and Restore Purchases in the TestFlight sandbox; no real charge should occur.
• Check consent choices and eligible test ads on a physical iPhone.

Known release limitation: this build uses test advertising. Production advertising and final privacy configuration require separate release preparation.
