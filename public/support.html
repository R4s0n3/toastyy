Contact legal@miomideal.com for support. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or payment details.

How to play

Turn the knob to choose difficulty and pull the lever. Wait for the orange button to change from WAIT to STOP, then tap the screen or STOP before the toast burns. Only the final 720 milliseconds accept a stop. Early taps keep the toaster running and earn nothing.

Save a slice to earn XP; a burnt slice earns 5 XP. DAYDREAM unlocks at 1,000,000 total XP.

Sound and haptics

Toggle them independently in Settings. Sound follows your iPhone’s silent mode. Haptics require a supported physical device. The app respects Reduce Motion.

Restore Remove Ads

Open Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Contact Apple for billing and refunds.

Your XP and records are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove this progress. Restoring a purchase restores the ad-free entitlement, not your XP.

Advertising and privacy

The game remains playable when an ad cannot load. Where required, Settings includes Advertising Privacy Choices. To report an inappropriate ad, use its reporting controls or contact support with the advertiser name, approximate time and a screenshot.
